A phone can join any room. Usually it joins to **ask**: it sends questions and reads answers while the computers run the model. A phone alone can't run a model, so start the room on a computer.

## Join from a phone

Scan the room's QR code with the camera, or open the invite link. See [Invite devices](/docs/invite).

You need:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Safari on iOS 26 or later. Older iOS can join to chat.
- **Android:** Chrome 121 or later.

## Why phones usually only ask

A phone's GPU is much slower than a computer's, and every device the answer passes through adds a Wi-Fi hop. So the room gives layers to the fastest devices first. A phone gets layers only when the computers don't have enough memory. See [How layers are split](/docs/rooms/split).

## How much a phone can give

The browser tab limits it, not the GPU.

  <TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
    0.5 GB by default, at most 1 GB.

  <TabItem label="Android">
    | Phone memory | Most it can give |
    |---|---|
    | up to 2 GB | 0.5 GB |
    | below 8 GB | 1 GB |
    | 8 GB or more | 2 GB |
    | not reported | 1 GB |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Keep a phone serving

If your phone does hold layers:

- **Keep the room's tab in front.** A background tab can stall the whole room.
- **Keep the screen on.** Pooled tries to keep it awake. If it can't, it says so: set Auto-Lock to **Never** on an iPhone, or the screen timeout to its longest on Android.
- **Plug it in.** Serving drains the battery fast.

## If a phone locks or reloads

- **Back within a minute:** it reconnects and the answer carries on.
- **Reloaded:** it rejoins under the same name and gets its layers back.
- **Gone for over a minute:** the room deals its layers to the other devices and keeps going. This is the **Re-deal on its own** setting, on by default.

See [Device drops and recovery](/docs/rooms/recovery) for the details, and [Benchmarks](/docs/internals/benchmarks) for phone speed measurements.
