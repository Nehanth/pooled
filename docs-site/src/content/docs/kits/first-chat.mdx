**Goal:** ask an AI model a question, running on your own laptop, in a browser tab.

## You need

- **One laptop or desktop** with Chrome or Edge (113 or newer), or Safari 26. See [Browsers and requirements](/docs/browsers) for Linux and Firefox.
- **About 23 GB of free GPU memory** for the Qwen3.6 35B MoE, the model in this guide (the room says it needs 22.5 GB).
- **10 minutes**, and a **19.4 GB download** the first time (under 6 minutes on a fast line in our test). Later starts use the copy the browser keeps.

On a small laptop, pick Qwen3 1.7B (4 GB at 8K), or pool it with another device: see [Pool two devices](/docs/kits/two-devices).

No account and nothing to install.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Open [pooled.run/room](https://pooled.run/room).**

   The page checks that the browser can use the GPU. If it can't, it says so and how to fix it.

   <Shot src={start} alt="The start screen: a name for this device, how much memory it gives, and the Start a room button" />

2. **Check the two fields at the top.**

   **This device** is the name others will see. **Memory** is how many GB this laptop gives the room. It starts at 4 GB: raise it to **24** for the MoE. You can change it later in the room.

3. **Press Start a room.**

   You are in a room of one. The room code, like `4TK-G9P`, shows in the header. You only need it when you add devices.

4. **Pick Qwen3.6 35B MoE.**

   **Pick a model** lists the three models. Each one says **fits**, or how many GB the room is short, like `13 GB short`. Pick **Qwen3.6 35B MoE**: it needs 22.5 GB, and the room says `Needs 22.5 GB. The room has 24 GB.` On the right, **The room pools** shows what this laptop gives.

   With the 1.7B on a small laptop, 4 to 5.5 GB gives an 8K context: the row says `4 GB · 8K` and **fits**, and the room says `Qwen3 1.7B · 8K context: the room's memory is short for 16K`.

   <Shot src={picker} alt="The model picker with Qwen3.6 35B MoE chosen: it needs 22.5 GB and fits, and the room pools 24 GB from this laptop" />

5. **Press Start the model.**

   A card shows the download and then the load, with the GB done and the time left. On a fast connection this takes a few minutes (5.5 minutes in our test, for the 19.4 GB download). When it's done, the room says **Model ready on 1 device** and the message box at the bottom turns on.

   <Shot src={loading} alt="The Loading Qwen3.6 35B MoE card: this laptop downloading layers 1 to 40, 1.9 GB of 19.4 GB, about 10 minutes left" caption="The load card while the model downloads." />

6. **Ask something.**

   Type in **Message the room**, for example `Explain what a GPU is in two sentences.`, and press **Send**. The answer streams in word by word.

   <Shot src={answer} alt="The room with Qwen3.6 35B MoE Ready, the question, a two-sentence answer and the line 73 tok, 25.7 tok/s, 1 device, 48% drafts accepted" caption="Ready on one laptop, with the first answer. The bar shows the layers this laptop holds." />

</Steps>

## What you should see

- After step 3: the room screen. The header shows the room code, **Invite** and how many GB the room pools.
- After step 5: **Qwen3.6 35B MoE** and **Ready** at the top, with a bar of the 40 layers this laptop holds and a 32k context.
- After step 6: an answer within a few seconds, and under it a line like `73 tok · 25.7 tok/s · 1 device · 48% drafts accepted`. The smaller 1.7B writes about 20 to 50 tokens a second on a recent laptop.

Keep the tab in front and the laptop plugged in while you use it. A background tab or a sleeping screen can stall the model.

## If something goes wrong

### "This device can chat but not help run the model"
The browser has no usable WebGPU. On Linux, Chrome needs `chrome://flags/#enable-unsafe-webgpu` and `chrome://flags/#enable-vulkan` turned on. On a Mac, update to Safari 26 or use Chrome. The join screen shows the fix for your browser under **Details**.

### Start the model stays grey
The model says the room is short, for example `2 GB short`. Raise **This device gives**, or press **Give N GB from this device**. If the laptop can't spare 23 GB, pick Qwen3 1.7B, or add a second device: see [Pool two devices](/docs/kits/two-devices).

### "Can't reach Hugging Face" while it downloads
The model comes from Hugging Face. A VPN, an ad blocker or a firewall can block it. Fix that and press **Start the model** again: the parts that already downloaded are kept.

More messages and fixes: [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
