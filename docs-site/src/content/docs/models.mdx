Pick by how much memory the room has. The header shows it as `N GB pooled`, and the picker preselects the biggest model that fits.

| Model | The room needs | Download | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3 1.7B | 5.6 GB (4 GB at 8K) | 1.83 GB | 16K (8K when the room is short) |
| Qwen3.8 27B | 17 GB (16.5 GB at 8K) | 16.06 GB | up to 64K: the longest the room holds (16K at 17 GB, 64K at about 20 GB) |
| Qwen3.6 35B MoE | 22.5 GB (22 GB at 8K) | 20.84 GB | up to 128K: the longest the room holds (32K at 22.5 GB, 128K at about 23.5 GB) |
| Qwen3.5 122B MoE (preview: `?dev=1`, or the terminal) | 73 GB | 72.52 GB | 32K (up to 128K) |
| Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (**experimental**; `POOLED_DEV=1` for `pooled host` / `list`) | 72 GB, or about 8 GB on one tab with its experts on disk | 100.6 GB (71.8 GB of layers, 28.8 GB n-gram table) | 64K (the picker offers 8K, 32K and 64K; 128K in progress) |

The download is split across the devices that run the model, so each one fetches only its part.

The 27B and the MoE open at the longest context the room holds without slowing down (see [Longer context](#longer-context)). The 1.7B opens with a 16K context when the room has 5.6 GB for it. With less, down to 4 GB, it opens with 8K instead, and the room says so: `Qwen3 1.7B · 8K context: the room's memory is short for 16K`. The picker row then reads `4 GB · 8K fits`. Add a device or raise a pledge for 16K. A room opened with `?ctx=` keeps the context it asked for.

## Which one to pick

- **One laptop:** Qwen3 1.7B.
- **17 to 22 GB across the room:** Qwen3.8 27B.
- **22.5 GB or more:** Qwen3.6 35B MoE. It is the bigger file but the fastest of the two big models, because each word uses only a small part of it.
- **Claude Code or Codex through the [Serve API](/docs/serve):** the MoE or the 27B. Their prompts alone are too long for the 1.7B.
- **One PC with a 12 GB GPU:** the MoE from the terminal, with `pooled host qwen3.6-35b-moe --here`. It keeps the experts the GPU can't hold in system RAM (about 19 GB of them) and runs at about 38-45 tok/s on an RTX 5070. See [MoE experts in system RAM](/docs/terminal#moe-experts-in-system-ram).

In the terminal, `pooled host` and `pooled pull` also list the **Qwen3.5 122B MoE**: about 73 GB across the room and a 72.52 GB download. A Mac Studio lending 25 GB and an RTX 5070 PC keeping 42 GB of its experts in RAM run it at about 17 tok/s. The room page shows it only with `?dev=1`.

When a room's GPUs and RAM together still fall short of a MoE model, a computer started with `--disk` can read some of its experts from the model's files on its SSD instead. The room does this only as a last resort, and it is slower. See [MoE experts read from disk](/docs/terminal#moe-experts-read-from-disk). A browser tab has its own disk tier for Qwen3.8 Flash (below).

Phones add little memory: 0.5 to 2 GB each. See [Phones and tablets](/docs/phones).

## Qwen3.8 Flash (experimental)

  <source src="/docs/media/flash-demo.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
</video>

*Recorded October 13, 2026 (2:13, subtitles): Qwen3.8 Flash in a Chrome tab on a Mac Studio, then an RTX 5070 PC joins the room and it gets 1.4x faster.*

Qwen3.8 Flash is a 125B mixture-of-experts model that uses about 6B of its parameters per token. It is far bigger than most GPUs' memory, so Pooled runs it from disk:

- Each device keeps its share of the model's experts on its SSD: in the browser's storage (OPFS) for a tab, after you allow it, or in the model files for `pooled join`. A tab copies its share once, up to 92.4 GB.
- Each device keeps the experts it used recently in a cache on its GPU. A small cache serves most lookups, because routing repeats: on recorded traces, about 17% of a layer's experts serve about 77% of lookups.
- The rest are read from disk as a token needs them.

Reading from disk is the slow part. Every device that joins holds part of the model in its own memory, so less comes from disk and the room gets faster. A Mac Studio running it alone in a Chrome tab decodes about 8 tokens/s; with an RTX 5070 PC joined, about 10-12 tokens/s, depending on the network between them.

It is **experimental**. Context goes up to 64K tokens (the picker offers 8K, 32K and 64K), tested against llama.cpp; 128K is in progress. To lend or host it from the terminal, run the CLI from the repo for now: the CLI on npm doesn't have Flash's newest code yet (see the [CLI README](https://github.com/Nehanth/pooled/blob/main/cli/README.md#qwen38-flash-experimental-run-the-cli-from-the-repo)).

:::tip
Only give memory the device really has free. On a Mac, the GPU shares memory with everything else that is open.
:::

## Longer context

Context is the prompt and the answer together. The 27B, the MoE and Qwen3.8 Flash open at the longest context the room's memory holds, so you don't need to set anything:

| Model | Lengths | Opens at |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.8 27B | 8K, 16K, 32K, 64K | the longest that fits, at least 16K when the room holds that |
| Qwen3.6 35B MoE | 8K, 16K, 32K, 64K, 128K | the longest that fits, at least 32K when the room holds that |
| Qwen3.8 Flash | 2K to 64K | the longest that fits |

A longer context only takes memory: a reply comes just as fast at 128K as at 32K until the conversation actually gets that long. So the room goes past the 27B's 16K or the MoE's 32K only when that costs no speed: every device keeps its layers' experts on its GPU, and no extra device (such as a phone) has to hold layers. Otherwise it stays at 16K or 32K. A room short of memory even for those opens at a shorter length and says so, for example `Qwen3.6 35B MoE · 8K context: the room's memory is short for 32K`.

The picker shows a **Context** row under the models with each length, the memory it needs in this room, and which ones fit. Pick one to open the room at it (the room remembers your choice). While the model runs, **Room settings** can change it; the model then loads again on every device. Reading a long prompt from scratch still takes a while: about 2 minutes for 32K tokens and 15 minutes for 128K on the MoE on one GB10.

To ask for an exact length instead, add `?ctx=` to the room's address before you press Start, on the device that will host. For example, 64K on the MoE:

```text
https://pooled.run/room?ctx=65536
```

The room then opens at that length or not at all. See [URL options](/docs/reference/url-options) for this and `?kv=q8`, which saves memory on the two big models.
