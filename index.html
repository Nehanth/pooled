    YOUR SESSION, EXPLAINED

    A little transparency.

    No account required

    Chat is held in tab memory and disappears on reload. Saved notes use local browser storage. There is no cross-device sync or account recovery.

    Where computation happens

    Local uses this device’s GPU. Model downloads contact Hugging Face; your prompts are not included in those requests. No inference API receives your conversations. Loading may fail on unsupported browsers or devices with insufficient memory.

    Trusted rooms

    Rooms use signaling and may use relays. Admitted inference devices can learn room data. Invite links grant bearer access; codes require host approval by default. A room is not an ownership-enforced Private Net.

    What is still planned

    Private Net ownership enforcement, Global Net, credits, service connectors, microphone conversation and cross-device durable memory are not implemented.
